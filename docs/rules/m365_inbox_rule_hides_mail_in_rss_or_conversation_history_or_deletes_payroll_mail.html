Inbox Rule Moving Mail To RSS Or Conversation History Folder Or Deleting Payroll Mail

HighExperimentalMicrosoft 365 · audit
Download .yml View on GitHub

What it detects

Detects creation or update of an Exchange Online inbox rule that moves messages to the RSS Feeds, RSS Subscriptions or Conversation History folder, or that deletes messages matching payroll, direct deposit, bank or bonus keywords.

Why this rule exists

Huntress reports business email compromise after token replay in which hidden inbox rules with short or punctuation-only names used the actions "Move to RSS Feeds, mark as read", "Move to RSS Subscriptions, mark as read", "Move to Conversation History, mark as read" and "Mark as read, delete", with the condition "Subject or body contains payroll, direct deposit, bank, or bonus". In four mailboxes, "rules were created through the Outlook REST API rather than Outlook itself"; rules created outside the Exchange cmdlets can be logged as UpdateInboxRules (with OperationProperties) instead of New-InboxRule/Set-InboxRule, so both are covered.

Note: UpdateInboxRules may record the destination folder as a folder ID instead of a display name, in which case the folder-name branch will not fire; validate against tenant data.

The report gives no unified audit log operation or parameter names. The operations New-InboxRule and Set-InboxRule with the Parameters field and the DeleteMessage value follow SigmaHQ microsoft365_susp_inbox_rule_creation_or_update_activity.yml, and UpdateInboxRules with the OperationProperties field follows SigmaHQ microsoft365_susp_email_forwarding_activity.yml. Matching DeleteMessage inside OperationProperties is not used by SigmaHQ and is unverified; UpdateInboxRules may encode the delete action differently, so the REST delete branch may not fire.

This rule narrows that generic SigmaHQ hunting logic to the folders and keywords from the report.

falsepositives:

False positives & tuning

  • Users who deliberately file newsletters or Teams/Skype conversation notifications into the RSS Feeds or Conversation History folder with their own rule; review the rule name and conditions with the mailbox owner
  • Users or finance staff who auto-delete bank or bonus marketing mail with a keyword rule; check whether the rule name is meaningful and whether the creating session matches the user's normal devices
